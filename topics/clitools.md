## Move from "West vs East" to "Polycentric"

Many people unconsciously assume:

```text
West → produces knowledge
Others → receive knowledge
```

or increasingly:

```text
China → produces knowledge
West → declining
```

I would avoid both.

The world is becoming more polycentric:

* North America
* Europe
* China
* India
* Southeast Asia
* Africa
* Latin America
* Muslim-majority societies

all contribute something.

For example:

| Civilization   | Strength                                        |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Western        | scientific method, institutions                 |
| Chinese        | long-term thinking, relational networks         |
| Indian         | pluralism, philosophical traditions             |
| Islamic        | preservation of knowledge, community structures |
| African        | communal approaches and oral traditions         |
| Latin American | contextual theology and grassroots movements    |

As a Christian, you can learn from all of them without being owned by any of them.

## Learn From Muslim and Chinese Contexts Directly

One danger of postcolonial critique is that people continue to interpret other civilizations through Western academic frameworks.

A better path is:

* read Muslim scholars
* read Chinese scholars
* read African scholars
* read Indian scholars

directly whenever possible.

Ask:

> How do they describe themselves?

rather than:

> How are they described by Western academia?

Your linguistic training is a major advantage here.

Instead of becoming an advocate for one camp, consider becoming a steward.

## Revisit the Idea of "Mission"

Historically, missions and colonialism sometimes became intertwined.

Your concern is valid.

But it is also important not to flatten history.

There were missionaries who:

* supported colonial structures

and missionaries who:

* protected minority languages
* created writing systems
* translated Scripture
* defended indigenous peoples

The reality is mixed.

The future of mission may look less like:

```text
West → Rest
```

and more like:

```text
Everyone → Everyone
```

Chinese missionaries serving Africa.

Africans serving Europe.

Latin Americans serving Asia.

Diaspora communities serving globally.

This is already happening.
